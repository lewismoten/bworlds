WHAT IS THIS?

A world without a finished map.

KOGNABO is an experiment in building a world from systems instead of starting with a hand-authored map. Terrain, settlements, routes, weather, creatures, sound, music, and other pieces are intended to emerge from deterministic generation.

The same underlying world can eventually be experienced through different views: text, maps, simplified graphics, or a full 3D environment. The presentation can change without replacing the world.
