# Procedural Music Ending Motif Reuse

The lead motif restatement near the song ending now targets both closing
sections when they exist:

- `return` reintroduces the opening motif at the start of its first phrase
- `outro` reuses the same opening motif shape again instead of only inheriting
  whatever material survived from earlier sections

This keeps the ending more recognizably connected to the opening theme without
changing the earlier `A` and `A'` motif statement rules.

To keep that restatement audible in the generated full song, the first outro
measure now allows the full four-note lead motif before the normal outro
density thinning takes over.
