## Seasons

add a outer globe of stars in the sky that appear at night with procedurally generated constellations, where you can see faint connections between the stars that make up the constellations. The constellations will appear to change with where the sun rises each day throughout the year. The amount of daylight/night time should change based on the constellation position of where the sun rises - but the sun will always rise in the east. the constellation is therefore a globe of sorts that rotates. The time keeper on display should adjust the amount of night and day, based on the position of the constellation "globe". The time keeper should also have an outer ring that displays the constellations as they appear to rotate as sort of a "monthly" calendar of sorts. The time keeper may indicate the constellation and moon phase as a "date" represnting a "month" and "week" (moon phase) of the month. Provide the ability to jump between constellations similar to how i can modify the time. If time is frozen, then so are the constellations. During night time, the stars become visible.
