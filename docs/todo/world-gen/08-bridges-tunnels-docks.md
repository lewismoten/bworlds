# Bridges, Tunnels, Docks, and Ferries

## Crossing Candidates

- [ ] Create a transport crossing candidate plugin.
- [ ] Find useful road and rail river crossings.
- [ ] Find useful mountain crossing locations.
- [ ] Prefer narrow river crossings when practical.
- [ ] Prefer natural saddles and mountain gaps.
- [ ] Store vertical approach points on both sides.

## Bridges

- [ ] Raise terrain approaches when modest grading helps.
- [ ] Keep road bridge grades near six percent or less.
- [ ] Keep rail bridge grades near two percent or less.
- [ ] Allow rail bridge grades up to four percent.
- [ ] Support major river bridges up to five thousand feet.
- [ ] Support road overpasses from fifty to one hundred feet.
- [ ] Support trestles from one hundred to five hundred feet.
- [ ] Keep bridge geometry separate from terrain splats.

## Tunnels

- [ ] Find valid tunnel portal pairs through mountains.
- [ ] Keep road tunnels at two miles or less.
- [ ] Keep rail tunnels at five miles or less.
- [ ] Lower portal approaches when modest grading helps.
- [ ] Use bridges before portals when terrain is too steep.
- [ ] Keep tunnel geometry separate from terrain splats.

## Mode Consistency

- [ ] Never mix road and rail on one simple bridge.
- [ ] Never mix road and rail on one simple tunnel.
- [ ] Require bridge endpoints to connect to matching routes.
- [ ] Require tunnel endpoints to connect to matching routes.
- [ ] Warn about orphan bridge and tunnel endpoints.

## Docks and Ferries

- [ ] Create a dock placement plugin.
- [ ] Favor docks near settlements and navigable water.
- [ ] Allow docks on rivers, lakes, and coasts.
- [ ] Allow opposite banks to form ferry pairs.
- [ ] Do not require ferry docks to be directly opposite.
- [ ] Connect roads to useful ferry docks.
- [ ] Store ferry route metadata between dock pairs.

## Special Structures

- [ ] Create a lighthouse placement plugin.
- [ ] Favor dangerous coasts, channels, and islands.
- [ ] Create an observatory placement plugin.
- [ ] Favor high hills and mountain peaks with open skies.
